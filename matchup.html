Matchup efficiency is calculated relative to the season's FBS average: league scoring level + offensive strength above/below average + opposing defensive allowance above/below average. Expected drives combine each team's drives per game relative to the league average. Home field currently adds 2.5 points to the home team's projection. Win probability is currently derived from projected margin with a logistic curve and is pulled toward 50% when model confidence is low.
